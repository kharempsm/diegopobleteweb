About Me

Hi, I’m Diego Poblete. I’m based in Santiago, Chile, and I work at the intersection of MarTech, analytics and product-minded automation. Over more than a decade in digital marketing, I have helped teams turn customer signals into clearer decisions, more reliable attribution and better experiences across the journey.


In my recent work at LATAM Airlines, I have led scalable measurement initiatives spanning web, app, server-side and paid media. I design measurement frameworks, event taxonomies and data-governance practices that protect signal quality across platforms while respecting privacy and consent requirements. The outcome is a more trustworthy foundation for campaign optimization, attribution and executive reporting.


I connect GTM for Web, App and Server-Side, GA4, Firebase, AppsFlyer, Adjust, CRM and media platforms such as Google, Meta, TikTok, Microsoft, LinkedIn, Amazon and DV360. I also work with Google Cloud, APIs, Pub/Sub and automation workflows to reconcile data, reduce duplication and make insights available sooner to the people who need them.


My approach is deliberately practical: begin with the business decision, then build the smallest dependable system that improves it. That can mean a tracking architecture, a data-quality control, an API integration or an internal product that removes repetitive work. I increasingly use AI agents such as Codex and Claude Code to accelerate prototyping, engineering tasks and automation—always with review, testing and clear ownership.


I enjoy collaborating with product, marketing, data and engineering teams to turn complex technical work into an understandable roadmap. Curiosity, thoughtful experimentation and durable data foundations are the combination I rely on to create growth that is measurable as well as sustainable.


My MarTech Stack Includes:


Expertise

MarTech Architecture & Automation

I design connected measurement and activation ecosystems across Web, App, Server-Side, MMPs, CRM, media platforms and cloud services. I use APIs and automation to reduce manual work, reconcile signals and help teams act on reliable data.

Analytics And CRO

I combine trustworthy tracking, behavioral analysis and experimentation to improve acquisition, conversion, retention and long-term value. The work starts with the decision to improve and connects metrics, research and testing to practical action.

Measurement & Data Governance

I establish event taxonomies, data-layer standards, naming conventions and validation routines that keep data consistent across platforms. This prevents signal degradation before it affects investment decisions, attribution or reporting.

AI-Assisted Product Development

I use AI coding agents such as Codex and Claude Code to accelerate discovery, prototyping, implementation and test preparation. Paired with human review and strong measurement criteria, they help turn valuable ideas into useful internal products and automations faster.

Front-End & API Integration

I build and integrate lightweight web experiences, data flows and APIs that make technology easier to use. The focus is always practical: remove friction, improve observability and give teams a dependable path from idea to operational value.

Data Privacy

I ensure compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations by managing data subject requests, consent, third-party data sharing, and children's privacy. Through robust governance, transparency, and security measures, I help organizations protect user data, build trust, and mitigate legal risks while enabling responsible, data-driven marketing strategies.
